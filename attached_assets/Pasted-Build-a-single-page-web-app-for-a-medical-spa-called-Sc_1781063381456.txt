Build a single-page web app for a medical spa called SculptRx. It's a GLP-1 pre-qualification quiz. Use HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks.

DESIGN
Clean, clinical, and premium feel. White background, soft shadows, muted teal or navy accent color, sans-serif font. One question displays at a time with a smooth fade transition between steps. Progress bar at the top that fills as the user advances. SculptRx logo placeholder at the top as a text lockup in the accent color.

BMI LOGIC
Step 2 shows two inputs: height (feet and inches as separate dropdowns) and weight (lbs, number input). Auto-calculate BMI when the user clicks Next. Do not display the BMI number to the user. Use it only for internal routing: under 27 = disqualify, 27–29.9 = show Q3, 30+ = skip Q3.

QUESTIONS & ROUTING

Q1: "How old are you?"
Single number input field. Only accept whole numbers. If the value entered is under 18 → Not Eligible screen. If 18 or older → continue to Q2.

Q2: Height and weight inputs. BMI auto-calculates.
Under 27 → Not Eligible. 27–29.9 → Q3. 30+ → Q4.

Q3 (conditional, only shown if BMI 27–29.9): "Do you have any of these conditions?"
Checkboxes: Type 2 diabetes or prediabetes / High blood pressure / High cholesterol or sleep apnea / Heart or liver disease / None of the above.
Only "None of the above" selected → Not Eligible. Any condition selected → Q4.

Q4: "Do any of these apply to you?"
Checkboxes: Personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer (MTC) or MEN 2 syndrome / Currently pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning pregnancy within 2 months / Known allergy to semaglutide or tirzepatide / Severe gastroparesis (stomach paralysis) / None of the above.
Anything other than "None of the above" → Not Eligible. None of the above → Q5.

Q5: "Do any of these apply?"
Checkboxes: History of pancreatitis / Currently taking insulin or diabetes medication / Surgery requiring anesthesia within 30 days / Type 1 diabetes / None of the above.
Any selection other than "None of the above" → Needs Provider Review. None of the above → Likely Eligible.

OUTCOME SCREENS

1. LIKELY ELIGIBLE
Headline: "Great news — you may qualify for GLP-1 therapy."
Subtext: "Based on your responses, you appear to be a candidate. Book a consultation to take the next step."
Button: "Book My Consultation" (placeholder link).

2. NEEDS PROVIDER REVIEW
Headline: "You may still qualify — a quick review is needed."
Subtext: "Some of your responses require a provider to take a closer look before we proceed. We'll flag your responses so your consultation is focused and efficient."
Button: "Book My Consultation" (placeholder link).

3. NOT ELIGIBLE
Headline: "GLP-1 may not be the right fit right now."
Subtext: "Based on your responses, GLP-1 therapy may not be appropriate at this time. We offer other treatments that may be a great match for your goals."
Button: "Explore Other Services" (placeholder link).

All three outcome screens show this disclaimer in small text at the bottom: "This quiz does not constitute medical advice. Final eligibility is determined by a licensed healthcare provider during your consultation."

CHECKBOX BEHAVIOR
"None of the above" automatically deselects all other options when clicked. Selecting any other option automatically deselects "None of the above."

VALIDATION
Prevent the user from clicking Next without making a selection or entering a value. For the age field, reject non-numeric input and show an inline error. Show a subtle inline error message if any step is submitted incomplete.

RESPONSIVE
Fully mobile responsive. Card centered on desktop, full-width on mobile with comfortable padding.

PROGRESS BAR
Accurately reflects 5 steps total, or 4 steps if Q3 is skipped due to BMI being 30 or above.
